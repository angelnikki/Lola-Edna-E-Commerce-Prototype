Our Story

Made with Love from Lola's Kitchen

Lola Edna's Homemade Food started as a passion project — a desire to share the authentic flavors of Filipino homemade spreads with friends, family, and the community. Every jar is personally crafted using traditional recipes, with absolutely no preservatives or artificial flavors.

  • All products are handcrafted in small batches to ensure freshness and quality.
  • Sourced from trusted local ingredients — supporting Filipino farmers and suppliers.
  • No artificial flavors, no preservatives — just pure, natural goodness in every bite.
  • Proudly a Filipino home-based food business serving the local community.
